CYBR LIGHT / hybrid architecture laboratory

Experimental architecture lab — not the finished beauty renderer. Persistent-lighting mode measures unit-Lambertian irradiance, not the original primary materials. Beauty-reference mode traces the original material response. Neither mode uses a temporal denoiser or exposure clamp.

Beauty reference preserves the original materials, metal reflections, glass and ground. It accumulates while stationary and resets on camera motion; it is the quality target, not yet the accelerated beauty renderer.

Original renderer (unchanged)

Raster modes use separate lighting buffers. Diffuse reconstruction follows the primary surface. Optical reconstruction separately matches reflected and refracted endpoints; changed or ambiguous paths reset. It remains experimental: nested glass and rough reflections still show motion noise.

Drag to orbit · right-drag to pan · wheel to dolly. Coverage mode marks cache misses magenta; lighting mode traces those samples instead of inventing lighting. Scene changes rebuild the cache; camera movement does not.
